How to Play

Learn the simple rules of Nebula Match and aim for the top score.

Step 01

Flip Cards

Click on any face-down card to reveal the space object hidden behind it.

Step 02

Match Pairs

Find two identical cards. Matching pairs stay face up and award points. Consecutive matches build up your combo multiplier.

Step 03 • Bonus Time

Get Extra Time

Running low on time? Click the Bonus Time button to count fish in a picture from the Fish Game API. A correct answer gives you +15 seconds (1 time only per game).

Step 04

Win & Record Score

Match all pairs before the clock hits zero to complete the game. Save your score and see where you place on the leaderboard!
